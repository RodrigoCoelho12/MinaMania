## 📖 Overview
*MinaMania* is an action-packed arcade/roguelike game where you control a miner whose mission is to clear the mines of enemy creatures—stone monsters that have absorbed the local minerals. By defeating these creatures, you help preserve the mines and unlock their true potential. Enemies drop collectible materials that can be spent in the shop to purchase upgrades and become stronger.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Features
- **🔧 Upgrade System:**
  - **Weapons:** Increases damage, cooldown speed, range, and more.
  - **Equipment:** Enhances defense, movement speed, and health.
  - **Skills:** Unlocks abilities such as dash, shield, extra life, and magnet.
- **👾 Enemy Waves:**
  - Enemies spawn in hordes, each representing different minerals.
  - As you defeat waves, enemies become stronger and more challenging.
- **🏆 Leaderboard System:**
  - Player scores are recorded and updated at the end of each match.

## 🚀 Key Features
✅ **Simple Controls:** Easy to learn, hard to master.  
✅ **Exponential Growth:** Start weak but get stronger as you collect materials and purchase upgrades.  
✅ **Massive Enemy Waves:** Face increasingly difficult hordes of stone monsters.  
✅ **Leaderboard Mechanic:** Compete with other players for the highest scores.  
✅ **Stylized Visual Aesthetic:**
   - Primarily low-poly elements.
   - Simplified design for accessibility.
   - Vibrant textures and environmental elements.
   - Eye-catching visual effects (explosions, particles, and glowing elements).
